How it works

From blank canvas
to sparkling art.

Diamondistry works just like real diamond painting. Every square has a symbol, every symbol has a colour. Match them up and your painting appears.

Start painting →
  1. 1

    Choose a painting

    Browse the collection, filter by category and tap the heart to save designs you like for later.

  2. 2

    Pick a colour

    The colour list shows every diamond colour with its symbol and how many are left. Select one to start.

  3. 3

    Place your diamonds

    Tap or click each square with the matching symbol. Wrong square? It gives a little shake and nothing is lost.

  4. 4

    Finish and collect

    When the last diamond is in place, your painting joins your collection and the reward lands in your balance.

Handy tools

Small helpers for big paintings

Larger designs can have hundreds of diamonds. These tools keep it relaxing.

  • + −
    Zoom

    Zoom in for precision or out to admire the whole picture.

  • M
    Magnifier

    A loupe that follows your cursor. Toggle it with the M key.

  • ⤢
    Fullscreen

    Hide everything else and focus on your canvas.

  • ✓
    Auto-save

    Every diamond is saved, so you can always pick up where you left off.
