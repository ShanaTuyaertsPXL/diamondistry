About Diamondistry

Made for slow,

Our story

A craft we love, without the mess

Diamond painting is wonderfully relaxing: match a symbol, place a diamond, repeat, and watch a picture slowly sparkle into life. We wanted that feeling to be there whenever you need a break, on the sofa, on the train or during a lunch break.

So we built a digital canvas that keeps the ritual and drops the hassle. Every painting is designed to be satisfying to fill in, and your progress is saved, so you can stop and come back whenever you like.

What we care about

Three little promises

Calm by design

No timers, no pressure, no pop-ups shouting at you. Paint at your own pace and stop whenever you want.

Lovingly made designs

From meadows to sunsets, every painting is built diamond by diamond so filling it in feels just right.

A collection that is yours
