Privacy policy

What we collect when you play Diamondistry, why, and what you can do about it. Last updated: 11 October 2026.

Diamondistry is a fictional brand, designed and built by Shana Tuyaerts as a portfolio project. There is no company behind it and nothing is sold. This policy shows how a real launch would handle your data, and it describes what this demo actually does.

Who we are

Diamondistry is a portfolio project by Shana Tuyaerts, who is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: use the contact form on the portfolio website that linked you here.

What we collect

  • Your account: username, email address, password (stored encrypted), and optionally your first and last name, display name and the line shown on your dashboard.
  • Your profile photo, if you upload one. Without a photo we show your initials; we do not use Gravatar.
  • Game data: which diamonds you placed per painting, completed paintings, rewards, your diamond balance, unlocked paintings and favourites.
  • Weekly challenge: when you start and finish the challenge painting, so we can rank finishing times. Your display name and avatar appear on the leaderboard unless you hide them in your account settings.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details in server logs, kept for 30 days for security.

Why we use it

To run your account and save your progress (needed to provide the service you sign up for), to keep the site secure (legitimate interest) and to send password reset emails when you ask for one. We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics.

Cookies and local storage

We only use functional cookies: to keep you logged in, and a short-lived cookie while you reset your password. As a guest, your painting progress is stored in your own browser (local storage). Because these are strictly necessary, we do not ask for consent and there is no cookie banner.

Who else is involved

  • Hosting: a hosting provider in the European Union stores the website and its database.
  • Fonts: Bunny Fonts (BunnyWay d.o.o., Slovenia, EU) serves our fonts without logging or tracking.

Everything else, including smooth scrolling and the game itself, runs from our own server.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account details, photo and all game data are deleted straight away. Server logs are removed after 30 days.

Your rights

You can view and change your details on your account page, and delete your account there yourself. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, for corrections, for deletion, or object to how we use it, through the same contact form. The shared demo account is reset from time to time. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
